# GenAI Projects

A collection of projects exploring generative AI, prompt engineering, AI-assisted creative workflows, and digital storytelling.

## Featured Project

### 🎬 Somewhere She Belonged

**A GenAI-assisted animated short film about dreams, independence, and an unexpected bond.**

A 54-second stylised 3D animated short following a young woman who dreams of travelling, works towards independence, and forms a heartwarming connection with a stray cat.

**[Explore the project, prompts, and creative process →](somewhere-she-belonged/)**

- **Final film:** [Watch or download the MP4](somewhere-she-belonged/video/Somewhere%20She%20Belonged.mp4)
- **Alternative viewing:** [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1YGd4iAH9K1qxBmvj7nBlQP139f9_gTb7/view?usp=sharing)

#### Tools and Workflow

- **Visual development:** ChatGPT / OpenAI image generation, Runway
- **AI video experimentation:** Adobe Firefly, Google Flow, PixVerse
- **Video editing:** CapCut Desktop
- **Documentation:** Character references, scene concepts, and prompt files

## What This Repository Demonstrates

- Prompt development for AI-assisted visual storytelling
- Character and scene continuity across generated visuals
- Experimentation with generative image and video tools
- Evaluation and selection of generated outputs
- Video editing, pacing, and narrative assembly
