# Somewhere She Belonged

*A GenAI-assisted animated short film about dreams, independence, and an unexpected bond.*

## 🎬 Project Overview

**Somewhere She Belonged** is a 54-second AI-assisted animated short film that follows a young woman as she dreams of travelling, works towards building an independent life, and forms a heartwarming connection with a stray cat.

Through stylised 3D visuals, warm colours, and a nostalgic atmosphere, the film explores the courage to pursue your dreams and the unexpected companions we find along the way.

## ✨ Creative Process

The project combines AI-assisted visual development, prompt engineering, generative video experimentation, and desktop video editing.

1. **Story development:** Developed the narrative around travel, independence, and the bond between a girl and a stray cat.
2. **Character design:** Created character references and visual variations to maintain recognisable facial features, outfits, and the cat's appearance across scenes.
3. **Scene development:** Created visual references for the bedroom, rainy window, leaving home, saving money, meeting the cat, and the final train journey.
4. **AI video generation:** Experimented with generative video tools to turn selected concepts and references into animated clips.
5. **Editing and assembly:** Edited and assembled the selected clips in CapCut Desktop, refining the sequence and pacing and adding music to create the final short film.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **ChatGPT / OpenAI image generation** — visual ideation, character references, and prompt development.
- **Runway** — AI image generation and visual experimentation.
- **Adobe Firefly** — generative video experiments and animated scene creation.
- **Google Flow and PixVerse** — experimentation with alternative AI video-generation workflows.
- **CapCut Desktop** — video editing, clip assembly, pacing, and music integration.

*Tools were used for different stages of experimentation and production; not every generated output was included in the final film.*

## 🧪 Experimentation and Challenges

Some of the key challenges in AI-assisted filmmaking were:

- Maintaining character and outfit consistency across different scenes.
- Translating still-image references into natural-looking motion.
- Experimenting with prompts and visual references to achieve a cohesive visual style.
- Evaluating generated clips and selecting outputs that supported the story.
- Combining separately generated scenes into a coherent narrative.

The workflow involved iteration and experimentation rather than relying on a single generation.

## 🎯 What I Learned

- Writing and refining prompts for visual storytelling.
- Developing character references and scene concepts for AI-assisted filmmaking.
- Exploring the capabilities and limitations of generative image and video tools.
- Editing AI-generated footage into a short narrative using CapCut Desktop.
- Balancing visual continuity, pacing, atmosphere, and storytelling.
