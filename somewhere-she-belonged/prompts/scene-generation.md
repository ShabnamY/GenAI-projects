# Scene Generation Prompts

This document describes the visual direction and scene concepts developed for *Somewhere She Belonged*, a GenAI-assisted animated short film about independence, travel, and an unexpected friendship.

## Visual Style

**Base visual direction**

Polished stylised 3D animated feature-film aesthetic, expressive characters, soft cinematic lighting, detailed environments, warm colours, dreamy and nostalgic atmosphere, natural poses, and coherent visual storytelling. Use a consistent character reference wherever possible. Avoid photorealism and unnecessary visual effects.

## Scene 1 — Dreams of Travelling

**Scene concept:** The girl sits in her bedroom, surrounded by personal belongings and travel inspiration.

**Prompt**

Create a cinematic stylised 3D animated scene of a young Indian woman in her bedroom, looking at travel postcards and imagining places beyond her home. The room feels personal, cosy, and lived-in. Use warm, gentle lighting, expressive body language, carefully arranged details, and a dreamy nostalgic mood. Match the established character reference and home outfit.

## Scene 2 — Rainy Window

**Scene concept:** A quiet, reflective moment beside a rainy window.

**Prompt**

Show the same young Indian woman sitting thoughtfully near a rain-covered window. Soft raindrops run down the glass as muted daylight enters the room. Her expression is introspective, suggesting that she longs for something beyond her current surroundings. Use a polished stylised 3D animated-film aesthetic, subtle reflections, soft shadows, and restrained cinematic composition.

## Scene 3 — Leaving Home

**Scene concept:** The girl prepares to leave with her luggage.

**Prompt**

Create a cinematic scene of the same young Indian woman leaving home with a suitcase and rust-brown backpack. Her posture conveys a mixture of nervousness, hope, and determination. Include recognisable details of the home environment and warm natural lighting. Maintain her established facial features and travel outfit, with a polished stylised 3D animated look.

## Scene 4 — Studying and Saving

**Scene concept:** Working towards independence.

**Prompt**

Show the young woman studying at a desk with a laptop, books, notes, and a small savings jar. Her surroundings feel modest and personal. Use warm desk lighting and a focused expression to communicate patience, effort, and determination. Keep the composition clear and visually coherent, with the same established character design.

## Scene 5 — Meeting the Cat

**Scene concept:** The girl notices a small stray cat.

**Prompt**

Create a gentle outdoor encounter between the same young Indian woman and a small tricolour stray cat with black, caramel-orange, and white fur. The girl wears a light-blue top, white trousers, white sneakers, and a rust-brown backpack. She notices the cat and approaches carefully. The cat appears curious but cautious. Use natural body language, soft cinematic lighting, and a warm stylised 3D animated aesthetic.

## Scene 6 — Feeding and Bonding

**Scene concept:** The girl offers food and gently interacts with the cat.

**Prompt**

Show the young woman crouching to offer food to the small tricolour cat. She moves gently and patiently, allowing the cat to approach at its own pace. Preserve the cat's distinctive face markings and the girl's established travel outfit. Use believable movement, expressive but subtle reactions, and a tender cinematic atmosphere.

## Scene 7 — A Growing Bond

**Scene concept:** The girl and cat share a peaceful moment outdoors.

**Prompt**

Create a warm sunset scene of the same young woman in her red dress with tiny white floral dots, gently holding the now-familiar tricolour cat. Preserve her recognisable face, ponytail, and wispy bangs, along with the cat's distinctive markings. Use golden-hour lighting, soft background details, a peaceful expression, and a dreamy polished 3D animated-film aesthetic.

## Final Scene — The Train Journey

**Scene concept:** The girl travels with the cat beside a train window.

**Prompt**

Create a cinematic stylised 3D animated scene inside a passenger train. The young woman, wearing her red dress with tiny white floral dots, sits beside the window while gently holding the sleeping tricolour cat. A rust-brown backpack rests nearby. Outside the window, a beautiful sunset illuminates green hills, a lake, and distant mountains. Use warm golden light, a blue patterned train seat, expressive but natural character details, and a peaceful sense of hope, belonging, and a new beginning.

## Scene Continuity Notes

- Follow the established character references and outfits for each stage of the story.
- Keep the cat's fur pattern and facial markings recognisable.
- Maintain a cohesive colour palette and stylised 3D animated aesthetic.
- Use each scene to communicate a clear emotional beat rather than relying on dialogue.
- These prompts document the scene direction; actual generations may differ and may require iteration.
