# AI Video Generation Prompts and Workflow

This document describes the prompts, visual direction, and experimentation involved in turning the concepts and character references for *Somewhere She Belonged* into animated scenes.

## 1. Overall Video Style

**Base direction**

Create a polished, stylised 3D animated short film with expressive characters, cinematic lighting, soft textures, and a dreamy, nostalgic atmosphere. Preserve the established character designs, facial features, hairstyles, outfits, and the cat's distinctive fur markings. Use natural movement, subtle expressions, coherent environments, and gentle transitions between emotional moments.

Avoid photorealism, excessive visual effects, unnecessary particles, and sudden changes in character appearance.

## 2. Character and Scene Continuity

Use the selected character and scene references as visual guidance wherever supported.

- Preserve the girl's recognisable facial features and hairstyle.
- Match outfits to the appropriate stage of the story.
- Maintain the cat's black, caramel-orange, and white markings.
- Keep lighting, colour palette, and environments consistent across related shots.
- Prefer gentle, purposeful movement over exaggerated gestures.

## 3. Example Video Prompt — Feeding the Cat

**Scene:** The girl crouches to interact gently with the stray cat.

**Prompt**

Animate the provided stylised 3D reference image into a gentle cinematic scene. The young Indian woman, wearing a light-blue top, white trousers, white sneakers, and a rust-brown backpack, crouches near a small black, caramel-orange, and white cat. She gently offers food and carefully pets the cat as it responds naturally. Preserve both characters' appearances, clothing, proportions, and distinctive markings. Use subtle body movement, natural blinking, gentle camera motion, and warm cinematic lighting. Keep the scene emotionally tender and visually consistent with the reference. Avoid sudden camera movements, distorted anatomy, changing outfits, excessive particles, and unnecessary background motion.

## 4. Example Video Prompt — Final Train Scene

**Scene:** The girl travels with the cat beside a train window.

**Prompt**

Animate the provided stylised 3D reference image into a peaceful cinematic train scene. The young woman, wearing a red dress with tiny white floral dots, sits beside the train window and gently holds her sleeping tricolour cat. Preserve their appearances and the composition of the reference. Warm sunset light falls across the blue patterned seat as green hills, a lake, and distant mountains pass slowly outside the window. Use subtle breathing, gentle movement, and restrained cinematic camera motion. Keep the mood dreamy, nostalgic, and hopeful. Avoid character distortion, changing fur markings, excessive particles, and abrupt scene transitions.

## 5. Tools and Experimentation

### Adobe Firefly
Used for generative video experiments, including short animated clips developed from visual references and scene descriptions.

### Google Flow
Explored as an alternative video-generation workflow. Generated results were evaluated for movement, atmosphere, visual continuity, and suitability for the story.

### PixVerse
Explored for AI video generation and scene experimentation. Outputs were assessed against the intended stylised 3D look and character consistency.

### Runway
Used for image-generation and visual experimentation during the project's development.

## 6. Editing and Final Assembly — CapCut Desktop

The selected clips were assembled and edited in CapCut Desktop to create the final 54-second short film.

The editing stage included:
- Selecting and arranging generated clips into a narrative sequence.
- Refining pacing and scene order.
- Combining the story's emotional beats into a coherent short film.
- Adding music and polishing the final presentation.

## 7. Challenges and Iteration

The main challenges included maintaining character consistency, obtaining natural-looking motion from still references, avoiding unwanted visual artefacts, and selecting clips that fit the intended style.

The workflow involved experimenting with different tools and prompts, reviewing the results, and choosing suitable outputs for the final edit.
