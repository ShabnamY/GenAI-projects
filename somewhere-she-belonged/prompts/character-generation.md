# Character Generation Prompts

This document records the main visual descriptions used to develop character references for *Somewhere She Belonged*. The prompts were refined around character continuity, clothing, facial features, and the film's stylised 3D animated aesthetic.

## 1. Main Character — Home Outfit

**Prompt**

Create an original, polished stylised 3D animated-film character reference of a young Indian woman. She has warm medium-brown skin, a soft round-to-oval face, large expressive brown eyes, black hair tied in a high ponytail, and wispy bangs across her forehead. She wears a muted sage-grey top and a dusty-rose skirt. Her expression is gentle, thoughtful, and quietly curious.

Use soft cinematic lighting, appealing expressive features, detailed but stylised materials, natural proportions, and a warm, nostalgic colour palette. Keep the character design clear and consistent for use as a visual reference across multiple scenes.

## 2. Main Character — Travel Outfit

**Prompt**

Create a full-body character reference of the same young Indian woman, preserving her recognisable face, warm medium-brown skin, large brown eyes, black high ponytail, and wispy bangs. She now wears a terracotta-coloured top, dark-blue jeans, white sneakers, and a rust-brown backpack.

Maintain the same polished stylised 3D animated-film aesthetic and consistent facial features. Show the complete outfit clearly, with a natural standing pose, soft cinematic lighting, and a sense of youthful optimism and independence.

## 3. Main Character — Later Journey Outfit

**Prompt**

Create a mature-looking version of the same young Indian woman, preserving her key facial features, warm medium-brown skin, expressive brown eyes, and black ponytail with wispy bangs. She wears a red dress decorated with tiny white floral dots.

Her appearance should suggest personal growth while remaining recognisably the same character. Use a polished stylised 3D animated-film aesthetic, warm sunset lighting, gentle expression, detailed fabric, and a dreamy, nostalgic atmosphere.

## 4. Cat Character Reference

**Prompt**

Create a small, adorable tricolour cat for a stylised 3D animated short film. Its fur combines black, caramel-orange, and white patches. The face is predominantly caramel and white, with a small black patch near one ear, amber eyes, a white muzzle, a white chest, and white paws.

Give the cat a natural, gentle expression and soft, detailed fur while keeping its markings distinctive and consistent across scenes. Use cinematic yet warm lighting and an appealing animated-film aesthetic.

## Character Continuity Notes

- Preserve the girl's facial features, skin tone, hairstyle, and overall proportions across outfit changes.
- Use the home, travel, and red floral dress references for their corresponding stages of the story.
- Keep the cat's tricolour markings, amber eyes, white muzzle, chest, and paws consistent.
- Use reference images when supported by the generation tool to improve visual continuity.
- Treat generated results as iterative concepts: review outputs and refine prompts when details drift.

## Tools and Workflow

ChatGPT image generation and Runway were part of the visual-development and image-generation experimentation. Selected character references were then used to guide scene development and AI-video experiments.
